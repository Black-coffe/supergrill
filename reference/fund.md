# Lens: fund — investments, deals, startups, due diligence, IC preparation

Goal: interrogate like an investment committee would — before the real IC does. The user leaves with either a defensible thesis or a clear kill decision. Default hardness here: **hard** (money + irreversibility), unless the user softens it. Default level: **super** when a real cheque is on the table; `deep` for a first screen.

## Question tracks (IC order)

1. **The thesis in one sentence** — "we invest X in Y because Z". If it takes a paragraph, it isn't a thesis yet.
2. **Market & why-now** — TAM built bottom-up, not quoted from a deck. What changed in the world that makes this possible NOW and not five years ago? Who died trying earlier, and why won't that happen here?
3. **Moat** — what compounds? Tech, distribution, data, switching costs, regulation? "First mover" and "great team" are answers to a different question. What does this look like when a well-funded copycat appears in 12 months?
4. **Unit economics** — CAC, LTV, payback, margin at scale. Numbers, not adjectives. If pre-revenue: which assumption in the model is doing all the work, and what's the cheapest way to test it?
5. **Team** — why is THIS team unfairly suited? What critical skill is missing, and what's the plan for it? Founder red flags observed so far?
6. **Traction & evidence** — what would falsify the growth story? Distinguish: paying pull vs pilot theatre vs vanity metrics.
7. **Deal & terms** — valuation vs stage sanity, structure, liquidation stack, board, pro-rata, information rights. What term would make you walk away?
8. **Risks & pre-mortem** — it's 3 years later and this went to zero: write the post-mortem now. Which cause is most probable? What's the mitigation, and who owns it?
9. **Exit & fund fit** — realistic exit paths and multiples; does the cheque size / stage / sector fit the fund's mandate and portfolio construction (the local overlay may state the mandate)?

## Grounding (offer, don't force)

- **Evidence from an archive:** if a call or meeting archive is connected, pull what was actually said in calls with the company and founders instead of relying on memory; cite the transcript and date.
- **Deck, model, dataroom:** when provided, challenge every founder claim against them; flag every number in the deck that has no source; recompute any figure that can be recomputed.
- **Public comparables:** public filings, registries, and press for comparables and for the founders' prior companies. Confidentiality rule applies to web queries: search the category, not the deal name, unless the user allows.
- **Document output:** if the local overlay names an organisation document skill, offer to generate the investment memo / DD report / IC one-pager through it. The grill brief maps directly onto memo sections.

## Super-level document checklist

- Pitch deck (latest) and any earlier version (to diff the story)
- Financial model with assumptions tab
- Cap table, current and pro-forma post-round
- Term sheet or proposed terms
- Customer list with contract status; 2–3 reference contacts
- Cohort or retention data; pipeline export
- Founder references and prior-company outcomes
- Prior internal notes, call transcripts, screening memo
- Legal: incorporation, IP assignments, material contracts (if at DD stage)

Suggested stages for `super`: (0) recon report; (1) thesis, market, why-now; (2) moat and competition; (3) unit economics and traction, requires model and data; (4) team and references, requires references; (5) terms, risks, pre-mortem, requires cap table and term sheet; (6) exit, fund fit, recommendation.

## Output

Brief = IC-ready: thesis, market math, moat assessment, unit economics with flagged assumptions, team verdict, deal terms table, pre-mortem, recommendation (invest / pass / dig deeper — with the specific dig list).
